## 2026-11-14 · M6 review: stability tests, two groups instead of three, clearer charts
**Who:** Nguyễn Hoàng Minh (Claude Code, Claude Opus 5.5)
**Changed:** A check of M6 against section 4.6 of the brief found three problems. First, the three groups of the earlier version were not stable: when the features were rebuilt without one week, the groups changed every time (mean ARI 0.32). Second, the name "Middle channel gap" did not fit every food: pork belly has the smallest app premium of all 12 foods (+12%) but sat in that group. Third, the loadings were about 4% too large, because the PCA variance divides by n minus 1 and StandardScaler by n. M6.3 now rebuilds the features without each week, from each district alone and without each food, and the rule for k takes the k with the most stable groups among those with a silhouette within 0.01 of the best. The rule gives k = 2: five foods (water spinach, watermelon, onion, white tofu, red tilapia) where the app costs 53% to 95% more than the wet market, and seven foods where it costs 12% to 35% more. The code checks that the app premiums of the two groups do not overlap, so each name fits every food. Ward linkage finds the same two groups (ARI 1.00). The biplot names each arrow, and a new helper keeps labels off each other and off the arrows. The k means figure gained a third panel for the week test. The dendrogram colours the foods by group. The profile page now shows the premium food by food. The text of M6, the summary and the website were updated, and the site was redeployed.
**Files:** `notebooks/P10_Final_Team10.ipynb`, `data/clean/m6_features.csv`, `data/clean/m6_clusters.csv` (new columns `super_over_market`, `app_over_market`), `data/clean/m6_leaf_order.csv`, `report/figures/m6_pca.png`, `m6_kmeans_choice.png`, `m6_dendrograms.png`, `m6_segment_profiles.png`, `report/ai_log.md`, `site/index.html`.
